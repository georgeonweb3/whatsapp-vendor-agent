# Sales Kit - WhatsApp Vendor Agent

Built for George to sell this in Lagos, in person and on Instagram/WhatsApp.

## The pitch (30 seconds)
> You dey lose sales because you no fit reply everybody fast for WhatsApp. When ad bring 50 people at once, some go wait 4 hours, dem go buy from another person.
> I go set up a bot for your own WhatsApp number wey go reply price, delivery and stock in 5 seconds, even for night. E go send payment link and protect you from fake alert. Setup na N20k, then N10k per month. You fit test am now with my demo shop.

## DM script for Instagram vendors

**Opener (do not spam, target vendors who post daily and write "DM for price"):**
```
Hello [Name], I love your [specific product - e.g. Ankara gowns]. Quick question - when people ask price for WhatsApp and you dey busy, how long before you reply them?

I build a small WhatsApp assistant for Lagos fashion vendors. E dey reply price/delivery instantly and send payment link, so you no lose customer. I get a demo you fit chat now in 1 minute, no obligation.

Make I send you the demo?
```

**If yes:** Send simulator video/screen recording + DEMO.md transcript. Then:
```
That demo na Adaeze Styles, but yours go use your own products, your prices, your delivery fees.

Setup na N20k, monthly N10k for hosting and support. First step na just your price list - even photo of your notebook dey okay.

You wan make I set up yours this week?
```

**Follow-up (next day, if no reply):**
```
Hello [Name], just checking - I no wan disturb you. The offer still dey if reply delay dey cost you sales. I fit do your catalog free preview today, you go see how e go talk to your customers before you pay anything.
```

## Objection handling

**"I no get money" / "Too expensive"**
> I understand. One lost customer for a N35k dress don pass the monthly fee already. If the bot save just one sale per month, e don pay for itself. We fit start with pilot and you judge by sales, no long talk.

**"My customers like to chat with me personally"**
> Correct, and dem go still chat you. Bot only answer routine price/delivery/stock. When customer ready to buy or ask special thing, you take over. Bot na your assistant, no be replacement.

**"I no trust bot / technology"**
> Make we test am for your phone first. You go chat am as customer, if e no talk well we adjust am. Your number and your Paystack na your own, I no hold your money.

**"I already have WhatsApp auto-reply"**
> WhatsApp auto-reply only fit send one message. This one know your full catalog - different price for different product, delivery fee by area, and e create order + payment link with total.

**"Fake alert / payment safety?"**
> Na why we use Paystack link. Customer pay for Paystack, you confirm inside your own dashboard before you dispatch. Screenshot alone no count, bot dey tell you that rule for every order.

## First 10 customers plan

Week 1 - Proof (target: 3 pilots)
1. Balogun / Lagos Island: 5 fashion vendors in person, show demo on your phone live.
2. Instagram: 20 targeted DMs per day to vendors posting "DM for price" in Lagos/Abuja/PH. Personalize first line each time.
3. WhatsApp groups you are already in: one helpful post, not spam - "I built a free demo, who wan test am for their shop?"
Goal: 3 pilots at discounted setup (with George approval) for testimonials.

Week 2 - Convert (target: +4 paying)
4. Record a 30-second video testimonial from pilot 1 (phone screen + vendor voice).
5. Post testimonial on Alpha Toolkit + your WhatsApp status daily.
6. Ask each pilot for 2 referrals - offer them 1 week free for a referral that pays.

Week 3 - Systemize (target: +3 = 10 total)
7. Price goes back to full N20k. No more discount without referral.
8. Create a simple onboarding form: shop name, products, delivery zones, Paystack slug.
9. At 10 vendors: N200k setup collected + N100k/month recurring. Pause sales, focus on support quality before scaling to 20.

## Targets to approach
- Fashion / asoebi / shoes vendors on Instagram with 2k-50k followers, Lagos based
- Phone accessories and wigs vendors (high DM volume)
- Food / cakes vendors (same delivery price questions every day)
Avoid: vendors with no daily posts, no WhatsApp link in bio, or already using a big CRM.

## What to never promise
- Never promise Meta verification or that bot can use a personal WhatsApp number unofficially - live route is WhatsApp Business API / BSP only.
- Never collect a vendor's Paystack secret key, BVN, or bank login.
- Never guarantee sales increase numbers - say "reply in seconds, no missed routine questions".
